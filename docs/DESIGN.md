# Design principles

Every screen should make sense to a school kid or your mom the first time they see it. If it doesn't, the design is wrong, not the person.

1. **Say what it is, in words people use.** "Tell it about your business", not "Configuration".
2. **One main thing per screen.** One big button, and it says what will happen.
3. **Big and readable.** 18 to 20 pixel text, a typeface made for legibility (Atkinson Hyperlegible), strong contrast.
4. **Easy to reach.** Everything works on a phone, with a keyboard, and with a screen reader. Buttons are at least 44 pixels tall.
5. **Show, don't explain.** Try-it screens start filled with an example, clearly labelled as an example.
6. **Be honest.** Practice mode says it is practice mode. The helper says it is automated and hands off when it is unsure.
7. **Color means something.** Green means go, sunrise yellow means look here, dark teal means where you are.
